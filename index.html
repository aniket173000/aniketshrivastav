What I'm Building

Nideknil: LinkedIn, flipped.

An AI hiring platform I designed, built and shipped solo. Recruiters don't hunt through profiles; AI ranks the whole candidate base for any role.

Nideknil
Founder & Engineer · Jun 2026 – Present
Visit nideknil.in ↗

Screening every applicant with an LLM is slow and expensive, so Nideknil treats the LLM as the scarce resource. Cheap stages cut the pool down first, and only the top handful of candidates reach deep AI evaluation. Every score comes back explainable, factor by factor.

01 · INPUT
Job description, embedded
→
02 · TOP 500
pgvector HNSW semantic retrieval
→
03 · TOP 50
Cohere cross-encoder rerank
→
04 · TOP 10
LLM deep evaluation
→
05 · OUTPUT
Blended, explainable score
🎯 Candidate Ranking Funnel
Resumes are parsed and embedded at upload time, so a "rank now" request scales with top-k, not corpus size. Runs async on Celery + Redis, with SHA-256 resume dedup and a Redis cache for LLM responses.
🤝 Merit-Ranked Referrals
Verified employees open referral posts. Applicants are AI-ranked into a live top pool plus a waitlist, and referrals happen within a time-boxed window. No cold DMs, no popularity contest.
⚡ AI-Fluency Scoring
Take-home assignments are delivered through Claude Code MCP servers. Candidates submit session transcripts with a CLI that redacts secrets locally, and a Claude judge scores them on a weighted 8-category rubric.
📈 Nideknil Pulse
A B2B product that measures a whole engineering team's AI fluency, built on the same transcript-scoring engine.
🏊 Pools, Displacement & Reserve
Each job has a limited acceptance pool. Stronger applicants displace weaker ones, displaced candidates move to a reserve pool, and every status change triggers an email.
✉️ Outreach Agents
LLM agents that turn a hiring post into a personalized pitch or a full cold-email application, with multi-provider fallback (OpenAI, Groq, Gemini).
